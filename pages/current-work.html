Ongoing projects at IFAE & PIC - Barcelona

Population Analysis of Binary Black Holes

Statistical Modeling & Astrophysical Interpretation

Active

Investigating the demographic properties of binary black hole (BBH) populations through advanced statistical methods, with a focus on understanding mass-spin correlations and identifying potential subpopulations. This work combines cutting-edge machine learning techniques with traditional statistical approaches to extract astrophysical insights from gravitational wave observations.

Research Objectives

  • KDE with Selection Function: Implement kernel density estimation incorporating detector selection effects to explore correlations between mass and spin parameters. Aim to identify distinct subpopulations of binary black holes and characterize their properties.
  • Normalizing Flows: Apply normalizing flow models as a complementary approach to KDE. Compare results between traditional KDE and normalizing flows to assess robustness and identify potential systematic differences in population inference.
  • Astrophysical Modeling & Publication: Develop physically-motivated astrophysical models to interpret observed BBH population features. Prepare manuscript explaining formation channels, evolutionary pathways, and environmental factors shaping the observed binary black hole population.
2

PyCBC Workflow & Anti-Glitch Development

Pipeline Development & Machine Learning for Data Quality

Active

Developing robust data analysis infrastructure for gravitational wave searches through the PyCBC pipeline, with emphasis on glitch mitigation strategies. This project combines software development with machine learning applications to improve the reliability of gravitational wave detections and support IGWN computing operations.

Research Objectives

  • PyCBC Glitch Executable: Develop a dedicated PyCBC executable for anti-glitch analysis. Create tools and workflows that can automatically identify and mitigate detector glitches that could contaminate gravitational wave searches.
  • PyCBC Workflow Understanding: Gain comprehensive understanding of PyCBC workflow architecture to provide effective computing support for IGWN operations. Document workflow structure, dependencies, and resource requirements.
  • Machine Learning for Glitch Detection: Investigate unsupervised deep learning methods for glitch forecasting and classification. Develop models that can predict detector glitches or identify signals from noise, improving data quality and search sensitivity.

IGWN Computing Infrastructure Support

Grid Computing, Workflow Optimization & System Administration

Ongoing

Supporting the computational infrastructure for the International Gravitational-Wave Observatory Network (IGWN) through active participation in distributed computing operations, workflow optimization, and system monitoring. This role involves regular collaboration with the global IGWN computing team and hands-on technical work with HTCondor, containerization, and grid computing systems.
